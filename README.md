## 📱 Install it on your phone (free, no app store)

### Android (Chrome)
1. Open **https://calebmahoney-fmu.github.io/falling-cat/** in **Chrome** (or scan the QR code above).
2. Tap the **Install app** button on the game's menu.<br>
   No button? Tap Chrome's **⋮** menu → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen** → **Install**).
3. Falling Cat appears in your **app drawer** with its own icon. It opens full screen like any other app.
4. Open it once while online. After that it **works offline**.

### iPhone (Safari)
1. Open the link in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.

### Computer (Chrome or Edge)
Open the link and click the **install icon** at the right end of the address bar, or just play in the browser.

To uninstall, remove it like any other app (long-press the icon → **Uninstall**).

## What's inside

- **Four model cats**, from a twist-only "tuck and turn" cat to a flexible-spine cat, all driven by one physics engine that keeps the total angular momentum vector at zero.
- **Modes:** Challenge, Free drop (up to 30 floors), Zero-g practice, and **Watch the cat**, where you set the start and the cat rights itself while a narrator explains each move.
- **[The physics](https://calebmahoney-fmu.github.io/falling-cat/physics.html):** moment of inertia (the inertia tensor), angular momentum, tuck and turn, bend and twist (Kane–Scher), landing impact and air drag, with live calculators.
- **[Simulation results](https://calebmahoney-fmu.github.io/falling-cat/results.html):** engine validation, survival maps, an optimizer that finds better moves, high-rise falls and zero gravity.

Built with plain HTML/JavaScript and [three.js](https://threejs.org/). Nothing to install to run it from source: open `index.html`.
